Population Intelligence Architecture • Governance

Governance: Clinical · Financial · Operational

Intelligence is only trusted, and value only real, when three lenses oversee the same pipeline in parallel. Each lens owns a question; the Value-Based Executive Committee integrates their answers into go / no-go decisions.

Three lenses, one pipeline

Clinical
“Is it clinically sound and safe?”
Mandate
Safeguard clinical validity, patient safety, and standard of care across every archetype and intervention.
Owns / signs off
  • Signal selection is clinically meaningful
  • Archetypes are distinct & intervenable (Gate A)
  • Intervention fits the phenotype
  • Risk tiering & clinical outcomes / safety
Owner: Chief Medical Officer + phenotype clinical leads
Key artifacts
Clinical validation sign-off, care pathways, outcome & safety dashboards.
Financial
“Does it hold against our own cost?”
Mandate
Test every archetype against the cost of covered lives; ensure actuarial soundness and defensible return.
Owns / signs off
  • TCOC baseline per archetype
  • Matched / risk-adjusted comparator
  • Savings test, break-even & ROI (Gate B)
  • Budget reallocation from outreach to pilot
Owner: CFO / Actuarial & Finance
Key artifacts
TCOC baseline, comparator model, ROI sensitivity, reallocation memo.
Operational
“Can we deliver & measure it reliably?”
Mandate
Run the pipeline with data integrity and deliver & measure interventions exactly as designed.
Owns / signs off
  • Data pipeline integrity & privacy
  • Enrollment, staffing & vendor / CHW mgmt
  • Intervention delivery fidelity
  • Measurement cadence & data quality
Owner: Population Health Ops + Data & Analytics
Key artifacts
Data lineage & controls, enrollment tracker, measurement plan, run-book.
VBEC
Value-Based Executive Committee — the integrating body
Convenes the three lenses, resolves conflicts between them, and holds the go / no-go and reallocation decisions. No archetype advances a stage without VBEC ratification.
Membership
Clinical, Financial, and Operational leads + an executive sponsor. Chaired operationally by the Population Health Intelligence Lead — the single accountable owner of the pipeline.
Cadence
Quarterly portfolio review; stage-gate approvals as archetypes progress; annual actuarial re-baseline.
Decisions held
Authorize signals & cost inputs, ratify validated archetypes, approve reallocation, and continue / pause / retire.

Cadence, stage gates & continue / retire rules

Gate 1
Validate cohorts & definitions
Clinical + VBEC confirm archetypes and intervention definitions before any spend.
Gate 2
Approve cost inputs & baseline
Financial + VBEC sign off TCOC baseline, comparator, and reallocation.
Gate 3
Launch
Operational readiness verified — enrollment, staffing, data capture, measurement plan live.
Gate 4
Evaluate (3–5 yr)
VBEC reviews measured value against baseline; decides continue, scale, or retire.

Continue / pause / retire criteria

  • Fails either validation gate → returned to Layers 2–4 for re-clustering or re-pricing.
  • Enrollment or delivery fidelity below threshold → operational remediation before continuing.
  • Measured net value stays negative past the J-curve window → pause or retire.
  • Safety or equity signal → immediate clinical review, regardless of financials.
  • Comparator or cost assumptions drift → annual actuarial re-baseline.
Data, privacy & equity. Operational governance maintains data lineage, access controls, and PHI safeguards across the substrate. Clinical governance monitors that archetypes and interventions do not create or widen disparities — the “Limited Resources” phenotype and social-need signals are reviewed for equity impact at every stage gate.
