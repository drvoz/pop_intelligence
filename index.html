Value-Based Population Health • Reference Architecture

Population Intelligence Architecture

A repeatable pipeline that turns covered-lives data into clinical intelligence — archetypes that are validated in parallel by clinical practitioners and by the business evaluating its own cost of covered lives. Diabetes is the first instance; the method is condition-agnostic.
Instance 001 — Diabetes • ~40,000 covered lives → 2,400 classified → 6 archetypes

The pipeline — data becomes intelligence

0
Covered-Lives Data Substrate
Claims, clinical (labs, vitals, meds), enrollment/eligibility, and social-need (HRSN) data for the full covered population.
Diabetes: ~40,000 covered lives → 2,800 with diabetes → 2,730 adults ≥ 25.
1
Clinical Signal Selection
Domain experts choose the clinically significant parameters that carry meaning — the features the model is allowed to see.
Diabetes: A1c & glucose, blood pressure, disease duration, comorbidity, resource/social factors.
2
Archetype Discovery — unsupervised ML
Clustering finds like-groups on the selected signals and numbers them. Groups are sized and positioned by clinical complexity vs. population.
Diabetes: 88% of adults resolve into 6 distinct, intervenable phenotypes (330 unclassified).
3
Intelligence Enrichment
Each archetype is given a primary goal, a matched intervention, an intensity, and a cost signature — a raw cluster becomes an actionable strategy.
e.g., High A1c & Glucose → glycemic rescue → pharmacy + endocrinology (intensive).

The keystone — dual validation

4
Clinical & Actuarial Validation Loop
Intelligence is only trusted once both lenses agree. Either gate can send an archetype back to Layer 2/3.
Validated by — Clinical practitioners

Does the archetype make clinical sense?

  • Groups are clinically distinct and coherent (face validity).
  • Each group is genuinely intervenable.
  • The assigned intervention fits the phenotype and standard of care.
  • Risk tiering (high vs. moderate) is defensible.
Validated by — The business (finance / actuarial)

Does it hold against cost of covered lives?

  • Total Cost of Care (TCOC) estimated per archetype.
  • Matched / risk-adjusted healthy comparator established.
  • Savings test: Diabetes TCOC − (Comparator + Intervention).
  • Break-even and ROI computed per segment.
Trusted intelligence = clinically coherent  AND  economically credible. The business evaluates its own spend as part of the loop.

From intelligence to measured value

5
Targeted Action & Resource Reallocation
Dollars move from broad, low-yield outreach to phenotype-specific interventions sized by population and intensity.
Diabetes: 85% of patients sit in just 3 phenotypes — where targeted investment concentrates.
6
Prospective Measurement & Learning Loop
Baseline → launch → measure → evaluate over a 3–5 year horizon (J-curve). Outcomes refine archetypes and re-open validation.
Net savings = avoided TCOC − intervention cost  •  ROI = net savings / intervention cost.
↶ Measurement feeds back to Layers 2–4 — the architecture is a closed learning loop, governed by the VBEC.
